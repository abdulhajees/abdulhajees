## I build AI systems that have to work outside the demo.

I'm **Abdul Hajees**, an **AI/ML Engineer at Aalee** working across production LLM applications, agentic workflows, tool calling, model orchestration, backend systems and product engineering.

At work, I lead the engineering of **Aalee Assist**, an omnichannel AI support platform with human escalation. Outside work, I'm building **Aaptam**, an AI-assisted expense tracker designed around fast input, local-first records and an assistant that can actually act on your data.

I like owning the whole path from **system design → backend → AI orchestration → interface → deployment**.
